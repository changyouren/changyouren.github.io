Dr. Zhaobing Liu currently works at the School of Mechanical and Electronic Engineering, Wuhan University of Technology. His research interests are in robotics and control technology and advanced manufacturing. He has authored over 60 internationally refereed publications and served as reviewers for many scientific journals. Dr. Liu is ranked as the World’s Top 2% Scientists in Main Field-Engineering (Single Year from 2021 to 2025 and Career Long in 2025) by Stanford and Elsevier.
